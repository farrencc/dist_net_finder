WHY IT IS HERE
  The conceptual pivot of Session 1. On the left half of the workshop the
  examples were abstract graphs - a spring chain, a barbell, two moons. Here the
  census map is redrawn as a network with values on the nodes, and from this
  slide onward every graph algorithm in the workshop applies to Dublin without
  modification.

  Say it plainly when this is on screen: nothing new is being computed, the same
  data is being looked at a second way.
