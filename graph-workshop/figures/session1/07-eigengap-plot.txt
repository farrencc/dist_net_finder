WHAT IT SHOWS
  A single line-and-marker plot titled "eigengap". The x axis is the eigenvalue
  index 1 to 10 and the y axis is the generalised eigenvalue of the two-moons
  kNN graph.

  The curve starts at zero at index 1, stays very low through index 4, then
  lifts away and climbs steadily to about 0.054 by index 10. The rise is
  gradual rather than a single dramatic step.

WHY IT IS HERE
  The standard heuristic for picking the number of clusters: count the
  eigenvalues sitting near zero, and look for the jump where the spectrum lifts
  away.

  Show it as a heuristic, not an oracle. This curve is a soft, gradual rise
  rather than a clean step, which is the honest situation on real data - the
  eigengap is a hint, and if two people read a different k off the same plot,
  they are both allowed to. The notebook marks this figure cut-first: nothing
  later depends on it.
