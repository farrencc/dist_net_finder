  The result is a smooth, almost continuous gradient across the city: deep blue

NUMBERS ON THE FIGURE
  lambda_1 = 4.37e-16   (zero to machine precision - the graph is connected)
  lambda_2 = 0.06089

WHY IT IS HERE
  The barbell trick, applied to a real city, and deliberately run with the
  census values switched off. This vector knows only which EDs touch which. What
  it finds is the geometric long axis of Dublin - the north-east to south-west
  gradient - and its sign split is essentially "cut the city at its narrowest
  waist".

  Two things to draw out. lambda_1 being zero to 1e-16 rather than exactly 0.0
  is the numerical signature of a single connected component, and is worth
  showing so nobody panics later. And the smooth gradient, versus the barbell's
  clean plateau-cliff-plateau, is the tell that this graph has no strong
  bottleneck: the split exists, but it is not dramatic. That is exactly the gap
  the attribute weighting fills in the next section.
