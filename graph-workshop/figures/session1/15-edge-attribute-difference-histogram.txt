WHAT IT SHOWS

WHY IT IS HERE
  The unglamorous but essential slide. The Gaussian similarity
  exp(-(z_i - z_j)^2 / 2*sigma^2) needs a scale, and sigma cannot be guessed
  from nothing - it has to be read off the distribution of differences that
  actually occur on this graph.

  The notebook takes the median, 0.428, as the default. The tall left-hand bars
  are the reason the weighting does something useful: most edges will keep a
  weight near 1, while the long-tailed minority get pushed toward zero. Those
  few heavily-penalised edges are where the boundary will form.
