NUMBERS ON THE FIGURE
  SIGMA = 0.428 (median edge difference)
  Edge weights: min 1.57e-06, median 0.607, max 1.000
  lambda_2 = 5.433e-03

WHY IT IS HERE
  The synthesis of the whole session. Edges now carry a weight combining two
  things at once - adjacency (the edge exists at all) and similarity (how alike
  the two EDs are). The eigenvector of that weighted Laplacian therefore finds a
  boundary that is both spatially contiguous and socially meaningful.

  Watch lambda_2. At 5.4e-03 it is small but comfortably non-zero, which is what
  says the weighted graph is still in one piece and this eigenvector means
  something. The next figure shows what happens when that stops being true.
