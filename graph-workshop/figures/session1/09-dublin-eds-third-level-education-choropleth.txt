WHAT IT SHOWS
  A portrait choropleth of the Dublin City electoral divisions with axes turned
  off and thin white ED boundaries, titled "Dublin City EDs, third-level
  education (Census 2022)". Six quantile classes on viridis, from dark purple
  (low) through teal and green to yellow (high), with a legend boxed at the
  lower right headed "% third level".

  The spatial pattern is strong and legible without any statistics: a broad
  yellow and light-green band across the south and south-east inner suburbs,
  dark purple concentrated in the north and north-west, and a further dark
  pocket in the far west and south-west. The coastal spit to the north-east
  reads mid-to-high green.

NUMBERS ON THE FIGURE

    48.29 - 59.77, 59.77 - 70.27, 70.27 - 85.64
  Reprojected to EPSG:2157, Irish Transverse Mercator.

WHY IT IS HERE
  The first sight of the real dataset, and the attribute that every weighted
  computation later in both sessions runs on. Worth pausing on: the clustering
  is visible to the naked eye here, which is exactly why the formal tests in
  Session 2 need to be able to say something the eye cannot - namely how
  surprised to be.

  Also the moment to make the CRS point: the file is read and immediately
  reprojected to a metric Irish grid, because contiguity, distance and area all
  depend on it.
