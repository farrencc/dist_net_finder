WHAT IT SHOWS
  A portrait choropleth of the EDs on the magma colormap - black for low,
  through purple and orange, to pale cream for high - with a colourbar labelled
  "betweenness" running 0.00 to about 0.26. Title: "betweenness centrality".

  Most of the map is near black. What lights up is a small set of connectors:
  the brightest ED sits just north of the river in the centre-east, an orange
  chain runs east along the docklands and the south quays, a large rose-coloured
  ED sits out west, and a cream one lies in the north-west inner area.

NUMBERS ON THE FIGURE
  Betweenness ranges from 0.00 to roughly 0.26.
  Computed with nx.betweenness_centrality on the 162-node Queen graph.

WHY IT IS HERE
  A property of the geometry, not of the census attribute - this map would look
  identical if every education value were deleted. Betweenness counts how many
  shortest paths run through each ED, so it picks out the waists of the city:
  the divisions that bridge north and south across the river, and the elongated
  ones threading along the coast.

  It also has a job later. The ED with the highest betweenness is the node
  Session 2 releases its heat shock from, precisely because it is the
  best-connected place to start - the hardest possible test of whether a
  boundary can hold anything back.
