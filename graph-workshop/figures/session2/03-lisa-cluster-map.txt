WHAT IT SHOWS
  A portrait map of the EDs produced by splot's lisa_cluster, titled "LISA
  clusters, p < 0.05", with a boxed legend at lower right: HH red, HL orange,
  LH pale blue, LL dark blue, ns grey.

  A large red HH block covers the south and south-east. Dark blue LL areas
  occupy the north and north-west plus a western pocket. A handful of orange HL
  and pale blue LH divisions appear near the boundaries between those regimes,
  several of them along the river and docklands. Roughly half the map is grey
  and not significant.

NUMBERS ON THE FIGURE
  alpha = 0.05, 999 permutations per ED, seed = 7.
  Local Moran's I computed per electoral division.

WHY IT IS HERE
  Global Moran's I says clustering exists; it cannot say where. LISA runs the
  same logic once per ED and localises it.

  The four categories carry different meanings and should not be flattened: HH
  and LL are cluster cores, while HL and LH are spatial outliers - a high place
  among low neighbours, or the reverse. Their tendency to appear along the seams
  between the red and blue regions is not incidental, it is what a boundary
  looks like in LISA terms.

  Important caveat to raise here and settle in the next figure: this map runs
  one hypothesis test per ED with no correction.
