WHY IT IS HERE
  Introduces the graph heat equation du/dt = -Lu, solved exactly by
  eigendecomposition rather than stepped numerically. The same Laplacian that
  produced a partition now produces a dynamics.

  Flag the per-panel normalisation explicitly or the sequence is misread as mass
  being created - the totals are all 1.

  The real payload is the dark south-west at late times. The front is not simply
  radiating outward at an even rate; something is holding it up on one side, and
  the next two figures name what.
